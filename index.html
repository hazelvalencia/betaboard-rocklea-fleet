Add Additional Role

A unique Rocklea Fleet Position ID will be generated automatically (for example POS-RF-001). This role record will be created only in the selected backup pool and flagged OUTSIDE APPROVED BUDGET. It appears on the org chart while a person is assigned. If the role later becomes vacant, it is hidden from the chart but the Position ID, record and history are retained in the underlying data.
